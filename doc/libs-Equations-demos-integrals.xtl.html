programlibs/Equations/demos/integrals.xtl

Two continuous equations of machine learning, written as the papers print them. GELU, the activation of BERT and GPT, is x Phi(x) (Hendrycks and Gimpel, 2016), Phi the Gaussian CDF: 1/2 plus the integral from 0 to x of the density exp(-u^2/2) / sqrt(2 pi), so no improper bound is needed. The tanh form the frameworks compute is an approximation; the integral says by how much. Then the KL divergence of two Gaussians, the integral of p log(p / q) over a truncation (the tails beyond -10..10 are below 1e-20), beside its closed form. Run with "just demo Equations integrals".
