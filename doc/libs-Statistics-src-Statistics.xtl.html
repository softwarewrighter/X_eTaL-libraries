librarylibs/Statistics/src/Statistics.xtl

Statistics: statistics -- median, quantiles, five-number summaries, z-scores, covariance, correlation, a least-squares line, binned counts; builds on the standard Stats. Import it with an alias of your choice: "sx:" u_se< "Statistics". Put libs/Statistics/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Statistics/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

X_eTaL's standard Stats has the mean, variance (of the population), standard deviation and range: import it beside this one ("s:" u_se< "Stats"). Results here are Floats.
