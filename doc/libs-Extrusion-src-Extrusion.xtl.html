librarylibs/Extrusion/src/Extrusion.xtl

Extrusion: validated simple polygons, symmetric meshes and block glyphs. Import with "ex:" u_se< "Extrusion"; depends on Geometry and Vector3. Contours are Geometry's 2 by N Float layout, implicitly closed. Meshes are T by 3 by 3 Float, triangle/vertex/xyz, outward winding.
