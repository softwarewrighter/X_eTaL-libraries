librarylibs/Vector3/src/Vector3.xtl

Vector3: batched 3D vectors and rigid transforms. Import with "v:" u_se< "Vector3"; Matrix is a dependency. Points are N by 3 Float rows, right-handed xyz, y up, radians. Transforms are 4 by 4, acting on homogeneous column vectors.
