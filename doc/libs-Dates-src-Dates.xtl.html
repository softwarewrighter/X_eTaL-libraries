librarylibs/Dates/src/Dates.xtl

Dates: dates -- day numbers from calendar dates and back, weekdays, leap years, month lengths, ISO text, month calendars. Import it with an alias of your choice: "d:" u_se< "Dates". Put libs/Dates/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Dates/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

A date is three numbers, year month day (2026 10 3), or a 3-row matrix of them, one date per column; a day number counts days from 1970-01-01 (day 0), so dates subtract and add as numbers. The calendar is the proleptic Gregorian one, for any year.
