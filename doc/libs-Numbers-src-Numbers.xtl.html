librarylibs/Numbers/src/Numbers.xtl

Numbers: number theory -- gcd and lcm, primes, factors, divisors, digits, integer square roots, Fibonacci numbers. Import it with an alias of your choice: "n:" u_se< "Numbers". Put libs/Numbers/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Numbers/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

Whole numbers are X_eTaL's 64-bit Ints: a result past about 9.2e18 is an overflow error (big numbers are an ask, X6).
