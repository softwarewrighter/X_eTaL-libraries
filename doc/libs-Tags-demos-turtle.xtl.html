programlibs/Tags/demos/turtle.xtl

A turtle on a grid: its heading is one of four choices, its state a tagged tuple of a position, a heading and a count of steps -- parts of different types, used by name. e_num< names the choices, p_arts< the parts; both write plain definitions when the program is compiled. Turns and moves are tables indexed by the heading, not branches. Run with "just demo Tags turtle".
