librarylibs/Trig/src/Trig.xtl

Trig: trigonometric and hyperbolic functions not in the base language, written in closed form from its builtins (s_in, c_os, a_tan, s_qrt, e_xp, e_xpm1, l_og, l_og1p). The hyperbolic tangent is the builtin t_anh; Trig keeps no copy of it. Import it with an alias of your choice: "tr:" u_se< "Trig". Put libs/Trig/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Trig/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

Angles are in radians. Every function works on whole arrays, one element at a time, and its results are Floats.
