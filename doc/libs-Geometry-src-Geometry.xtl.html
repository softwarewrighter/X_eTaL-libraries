librarylibs/Geometry/src/Geometry.xtl

Geometry: plane geometry -- points as 2-row matrices, distances, polygon area and centroid, rotation, scaling and moves, convex hulls, pictures. Import it with an alias of your choice: "ge:" u_se< "Geometry". Put libs/Geometry/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Geometry/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

Points are a matrix of 2 rows, x over y, one point per column, as X_eTaL's []P_ATH takes them; a polygon is its corners in order. Results are Floats.
