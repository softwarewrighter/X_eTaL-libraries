librarylibs/Parsing/src/Parsing.xtl

Parsing: total parsers for macro authors: a notation in a string in, its pieces out or a refusal, nothing dropped. Import it with an alias of your choice: "pa:" u_se< "Parsing". Put libs/Parsing/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Parsing/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

A macro parses a notation written inside a string; the language outside the string is untouched (X_eTaL macros cannot change how source is read, MC10). A parser that uses part of its text and drops the rest gives a silent wrong answer (the Equations library once did). These parsers each use all of their text or refuse the call: a group must close at its last character, a reference must end at its bracket, a chain must have a value beside every operator; and a whole parse can be written back and compared with its text. Refusing parsers take the refusal's tag in their left argument: the macro's error code and the place to report at (left, right or call), as []R_EJECT takes them ("bad-equation right"), so a refusal reads as the notation's own error.
