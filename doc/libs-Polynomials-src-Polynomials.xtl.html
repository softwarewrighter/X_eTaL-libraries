librarylibs/Polynomials/src/Polynomials.xtl

Polynomials: polynomials -- evaluation, sums, products, derivatives, integrals, real roots, text. Import it with an alias of your choice: "py:" u_se< "Polynomials". Put libs/Polynomials/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Polynomials/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

A polynomial is its coefficients, highest power first, as APL's decode reads digits: 3 -2 1 is 3x^2 - 2x + 1. Results are Floats.
