programlibs/Equations/demos/cnn.xtl

One CNN layer as the paper writes it: Y[o,h,w] is the sum over c, r and s of X[c,h+r,w+s] * W[o,c,r,s], two 4 by 4 pictures (channels) through one 2 by 2 by 2 filter. One call gives the result and its LaTeX (a line between $$ is drawn as math in the live demo); the result is checked against the library's cross-correlation. Run with "just demo Equations cnn".
