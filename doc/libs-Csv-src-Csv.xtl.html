librarylibs/Csv/src/Csv.xtl

Csv: comma-separated values -- lines to fields (quoted fields kept whole), a table of texts, columns by number or name, numbers, and back to text. Import it with an alias of your choice: "cs:" u_se< "Csv". Put libs/Csv/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Csv/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

A table is a matrix of texts (Box Char), a row per line, its first row the header. Fields follow RFC 4180: a field in double quotes may hold commas, and a doubled quote inside stands for one.
