librarylibs/Combinatorics/src/Combinatorics.xtl

Combinatorics: counting and listing -- factorials, binomials, combinations, permutations, subsets, Cartesian products. Import it with an alias of your choice: "cb:" u_se< "Combinatorics". Put libs/Combinatorics/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Combinatorics/docs. Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file.

Lists come as matrices, one combination (or permutation, subset, pair) per row, in lexicographic order.
