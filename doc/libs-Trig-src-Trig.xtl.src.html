1⍝│ Trig: trigonometric and hyperbolic functions not in the base 2⍝│ language, written in closed form from its builtins (s_in, c_os, 3⍝│ a_tan, s_qrt, e_xp, e_xpm1, l_og, l_og1p). The hyperbolic tangent 4⍝│ is the builtin t_anh; Trig keeps no copy of it. 5⍝│ Import it with an alias of your choice: "tr:" u_se< "Trig". 6⍝│ Put libs/Trig/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Trig/docs. 7⍝│ Names with l: are exported; those under h: are private to this file. 8⍝│ 9⍝│ Angles are in radians.
