Added scan, an operator word for a recurrence (prev names the term before it, by gives its seed), built from p_ower since a local binding cannot self-recurse and the array machinery's reduce/scan are only a running accumulation when associative. prev is reserved outside scan; a scan must be its term's only binding; a prev-using body without a seed is refused.
